The first release: milestones 0 to 5 of the design. A standalone node or an active/standby pair, configured from one document with confirm and rollback, serving DHCP, DNS and time, with remote-access WireGuard, ACME certificates and rolling, signed upgrades.

- An unattended installer ISO, raw and qcow2 disk images, and the CLI for x86_64 Linux.
- `SHA256SUMS` signed with the project key, and SPDX SBOMs for the image and the CLI.
- The coordinator runs in its own enforcing SELinux domain.
